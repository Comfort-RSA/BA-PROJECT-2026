About the project

What is AI Stream Recommendation?

AI Stream Recommendation is a system built to help Grade 9 learners make better-informed decisions when moving into Grade 10, by combining their academic performance, interests and career goals into one clear, explainable recommendation.

Why this project exists

Choosing a Grade 10 stream is one of the first big decisions a learner makes about their future, but it's often made under pressure rather than with good information. Learners can struggle to decide because of:

  • Pressure from parents to follow a particular path
  • Influence from friends choosing the same stream
  • A lack of clear career information
  • Uncertainty about their own academic strengths
  • Not understanding which subjects suit which future careers

This tool looks at a learner's actual Grade 9 marks alongside what they say about their interests and career goals, and turns that into a personalised, explained recommendation.

How it helps Grade 9 learners

  • Understand their academic strengths across nine subjects
  • Explore which Grade 10 stream realistically suits them
  • Connect subjects with possible careers
  • Make a more informed, confident decision
  • Reduce uncertainty by seeing the reasoning behind the result
  • Check the recommendation against what their own school offers

How it works

Machine learning

A Random Forest model trained on Grade 9 marks across nine subjects, learning which mark patterns tend to fit which stream.

NLP chatbot

Reads what a learner says about their interests and career goals, and matches the language used against each stream.

Combined score

Marks and interests are weighted 60/40 and combined into one fit percentage — more reliable than either signal alone.

School-aware

When a learner selects their high school, the recommendation is checked against the streams that school actually offers.

Not a replacement for guidance. This tool gives a second opinion, not a decision. It does not replace your teachers, parents, school counsellors or professional career guidance — please talk to them too before choosing a stream.
